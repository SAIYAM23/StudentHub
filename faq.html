Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about StudentHub and college events.

Common Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a web portal that helps students access college events, announcements and campus information.

2. Who can register on StudentHub?

Only students of the college can create a StudentHub account.

3. How can I register for an event?

Login to your account and visit the Events page to register for available events.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. Students can update their personal information from the Profile page after logging in.

5. Can I participate in more than one event?

Yes. Students can register for multiple events based on eligibility and availability.

6. Who manages the events?

College administrators manage and update all event information through the Admin Dashboard.

7. What should I do if I face any issue?

Visit the Contact page and submit your query using the contact form.

8. Is StudentHub free to use?

Yes. StudentHub is completely free for all students of the college.
