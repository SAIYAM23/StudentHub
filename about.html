About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple web portal developed for students to access information related to college events, announcements and campus activities from a single platform.

Our Vision

To build an easy-to-use digital platform that keeps students informed about all important college activities.

Our Mission

To encourage students to participate in technical, cultural and sports events by providing timely information and updates.

Objectives

  • Provide event information.
  • Share important announcements.
  • Increase student participation.
  • Maintain student event records.
  • Improve campus communication.

Features

  • Student Registration
  • Student Login
  • View Upcoming Events
  • Manage Student Profile
  • Admin Event Management

Why Choose StudentHub?

StudentHub brings together event details, announcements, student information and campus updates in one place, making it easier for students to stay connected with college activities.
